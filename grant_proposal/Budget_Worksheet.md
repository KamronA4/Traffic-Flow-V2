# URI Undergraduate Research Grant Budget Worksheet

**Project Title:** Village: AI-Powered Municipal Traffic Planning Platform  
**Total Budget Requested:** $1,000

## Budget Categories

### 1. Cloud Infrastructure and API Services - $400

| Item | Cost | Justification |
|------|------|---------------|
| AWS Cloud Hosting (6 months) | $200 | Required to host the web application and database for municipal access during pilot program. Includes EC2 instance, RDS database, and S3 storage. |
| TomTom API Premium Credits | $200 | Enhanced API access for real-time traffic data collection beyond free tier limits. Essential for comprehensive municipal coverage. |

### 2. User Testing and Feedback Collection - $300

| Item | Cost | Justification |
|------|------|---------------|
| Municipal User Study Incentives | $150 | Gift cards ($25 x 6) for municipal staff participating in 90-minute feedback sessions and usability testing. |
| Qualtrics Survey Platform (6 months) | $50 | Professional survey tool for collecting structured feedback from pilot users. URI's free account lacks needed branching logic features. |
| Training Material Printing | $100 | High-quality color printing of user manuals, quick reference guides, and training handouts for 3 municipalities (Campus Copy rates). |

### 3. Conference Presentation - $300

| Item | Cost | Justification |
|------|------|---------------|
| Smart Cities Connect Registration | $150 | Student registration fee for presenting research findings at the premier smart cities conference. |
| Conference Travel | $150 | Transportation and one-night accommodation to Boston conference venue. *Note: Requesting department match for additional $150 in travel costs.* |

## Total Budget: $1,000

---

## Budget Explanation

This budget directly supports the core activities necessary for successful project completion:

**Cloud Infrastructure ($400)**: The Village platform requires reliable hosting accessible to municipal users 24/7. AWS provides the scalability and uptime guarantees necessary for government use. The TomTom API credits enable collection of comprehensive traffic data beyond the free tier's 2,500 daily transaction limit, which is insufficient for covering multiple municipalities.

**User Testing ($300)**: Meaningful feedback from municipal staff is crucial for platform improvement. The incentives ensure participation from busy professionals, while the survey platform enables systematic data collection. Printed materials are essential as many planning meetings occur in rooms without reliable internet or projection capabilities.

**Conference Presentation ($300)**: Disseminating findings at Smart Cities Connect provides valuable exposure to potential municipal adopters and industry partners. This conference specifically focuses on municipal technology adoption, making it ideal for this project. The travel costs are minimized by selecting a regional conference and sharing accommodation.

All equipment purchased (primarily software licenses) will revert to the supervising department at project conclusion. No funds are requested for meals, personal accommodation beyond conference travel, or salary.

## Cost-Effectiveness

This budget maximizes impact through:
- Leveraging free tiers of services where possible
- Using university resources for development hardware
- Focusing funds on external costs that cannot be covered otherwise
- Seeking department matching funds for conference travel

The $1,000 investment will enable development and testing of a platform potentially benefiting all 39 Rhode Island municipalities, representing excellent value for the research investment.
