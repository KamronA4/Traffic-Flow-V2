# Village: AI-Powered Municipal Traffic Planning Platform

**Student Developer:** Kamron Aggor, Computer Science, University of Rhode Island  
**Project Stage:** Working Prototype Seeking Pilot Testing  
**Grant Request:** $1,000 for 6-month development and pilot program

## Problem
Small and medium-sized municipalities in Rhode Island lack access to sophisticated traffic planning tools, forcing them to rely on:
- Outdated Excel spreadsheets and paper maps
- Reactive rather than proactive traffic management
- Limited data for making infrastructure decisions
- No predictive capabilities for anticipating problems

## Solution
Village is a web-based platform that democratizes access to enterprise-level traffic planning tools through:
- **Real-time traffic monitoring** with interactive maps and AI analysis
- **Predictive analytics** to forecast traffic patterns and identify trouble spots
- **Data-driven planning tools** for evaluating infrastructure changes
- **Automated reporting** for stakeholders and public communication

## Technology Innovation
- **AI Integration**: Natural language analysis makes complex data accessible to non-technical users
- **Modern Architecture**: Cloud-ready web application built with Python/Streamlit
- **Smart Data Pipeline**: Automated collection from TomTom with credit-aware management
- **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices
